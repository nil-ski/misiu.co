# Hello, nil.ski

This is a sample post, written the way Ulysses exports it: no frontmatter, just a title as the first heading. The date comes from the filename, the title from that heading, and the summary from this first paragraph.

## Why a new home

misiu.co served me well, but it was time for something lighter. The new site is built with Astro and generated entirely at build time — no servers, no database, just HTML.

> Writing should feel like skiing fresh snow: the fewer tools in the way, the better.

## How posting works

1. Write a sheet in Ulysses.
2. Export it as Markdown into `src/content/posts/`.
3. Commit and push. That's it.
